Why is it free?

MediMinder is built and maintained by a solo developer, not a company. There's no subscription, no ads and no paid tier right now — and that's staying true for at least the next year.

Because nothing is collected or sold, there's no data to monetize either. The honest answer to "how do you make money from this?" is: right now, I don't. It's a project built to be genuinely useful, kept simple enough for one person to run without needing ad revenue or a subscription to sustain it.

If that ever changes, it won't be by breaking what's already free — existing features stay free.

Frequently asked questions

Is MediMinder really completely free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no paid tier and no in-app purchases active. It's built and maintained by a solo developer with no ad or data-monetization model.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. MediMinder works fully offline — reminders, records, appointments and the journal are all stored on your device and don't need a network connection.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the app?

Since everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling removes it. Use the built-in backup feature to export your data to a file (including Google Drive) before uninstalling or switching phones.

Can I manage medicines for my parents or kids too?

Yes — add a profile for each family member from the home screen. Each profile keeps its own reminders, appointments and records, and you can switch between them in a tap.

Will the app always ring, even if my phone is locked or in silent mode?

MediMinder uses Android's full-screen alarm intent so reminders show over the lock screen. For the most reliable delivery, allow the battery-optimization exemption prompt the app shows on first setup.

What permissions does the app need, and why?

Notifications and exact alarms (to ring reminders on time), camera (only when you choose to photograph a medicine or report), and storage access (to save the files you attach). No location, no contacts, no background data collection.
